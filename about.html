About MedWise

Fighting Misinformation Through Education

We believe everyone deserves the skills to tell fact from fiction when it comes to their health. MedWise is a free educational platform dedicated to building health literacy for all.

Our Mission

Empowering Critical Thinking About Health

In an era of AI-generated content and viral social media posts, medical misinformation has become one of the most pressing public health challenges of our time. False claims about treatments, vaccines, and diagnoses spread faster than ever before.

MedWise was created to address this challenge head-on. Our platform combines interactive quizzes, expert-curated content, and research-backed teaching methods to help people of all backgrounds develop the skills they need to evaluate health information critically.

We don't tell you what to think — we teach you how to think. By understanding the tactics used to create and spread misinformation, you become empowered to protect yourself, your family, and your community from harmful false claims.

Our Values

What We Stand For

The principles that guide everything we build and teach.

Trust & Accuracy

Every piece of content is reviewed by medical professionals and backed by peer-reviewed research.

Accessibility

Health literacy is a right, not a privilege. Our platform is free and designed for learners of all backgrounds.

Empowerment

We give you the tools and frameworks to evaluate information on your own, fostering lifelong critical thinking.

Impact

We measure our success by real-world outcomes — fewer people sharing false claims and more informed health decisions.
